CMMC Compliance Colorado — FAQ

Does CMMC apply to all Colorado defense contractors?⌄
CMMC applies to all companies in the DoD supply chain — prime contractors and subcontractors alike. If your Colorado business holds or competes for DoD contracts that involve Federal Contract Information (FCI) or Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), CMMC requirements apply to you. This includes the entire defense supply chain — not just large prime contractors.
Do Colorado Springs defense contractors have different CMMC requirements than Denver?⌄
No — CMMC requirements are the same regardless of which Colorado city you're in. The level of certification required depends on the sensitivity of the data you handle and the specific contract requirements, not your location. That said, Colorado Springs companies near Space Force and Army installations often handle higher-sensitivity CUI, which can push them toward Level 2 more quickly than some Denver businesses.
Can Workplace IT provide CMMC services outside Denver?⌄
Yes. While we're based in Denver, we serve Colorado defense contractors statewide. Remote CMMC consulting works well for most of the engagement — gap assessments, documentation, policy writing, and virtual team training can all be done remotely. For companies in Colorado Springs, Boulder, Fort Collins, and other Front Range cities, we can also provide on-site support for physical assessments and hands-on technical work.
What CMMC level do most Colorado defense contractors need?⌄
Most Colorado defense contractors handling CUI need CMMC Level 2, which requires compliance with all 110 NIST SP 800-171 practices and a third-party assessment by a C3PAO. Companies that only handle FCI and not CUI may be able to satisfy requirements with Level 1 self-attestation. We help you determine which level applies to your specific contracts during the free gap assessment.
How do Colorado's Space Force contractors approach CMMC differently?⌄
Space Force contractors often handle highly sensitive technical data related to satellite systems, communications, and intelligence — which typically means CUI designation and Level 2 requirements at minimum. Some programs may have additional requirements layered on top of standard CMMC. Workplace IT is familiar with the specific compliance landscape for Space Force program contractors and can help you navigate both standard CMMC and any program-specific requirements.
