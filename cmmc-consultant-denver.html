What We Do

What Does a CMMC Consultant Actually Do?

A CMMC consultant guides your organization through the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification process — from understanding where you stand today to getting you ready for a third-party assessment by a C3PAO.

Unlike a C3PAO (who performs the official assessment), a CMMC consultant works on your side — identifying gaps, implementing controls, writing documentation, and making sure you're prepared before the official assessment begins. Think of us as your compliance coach.

Workplace IT goes further than most consultants — we're also a managed IT provider, which means we don't just tell you what to fix. We fix it. Technical controls, policy documentation, employee training, and ongoing monitoring are all things we do in-house.

  • CMMC gap assessment against all 110 NIST 800-171 controls
  • System Security Plan (SSP) authoring and maintenance
  • Plan of Action & Milestones (POA&M) development
  • Policy and procedure documentation across all 14 domains
  • Technical control implementation — MFA, encryption, monitoring
  • Mock assessments before your official C3PAO visit
  • Ongoing compliance monitoring post-certification

Why Local Matters

A Denver CMMC Consultant Who Can Be On-Site

Most CMMC consultants work entirely remotely. That works fine for documentation — but CMMC also requires physical security assessments, on-site interviews, and hands-on technical work that remote-only consultants can't do well.

🏠
Physical Security Assessments

CMMC Level 2 includes physical protection controls. We can visit your Denver facility, assess your physical security posture, and document what we find — something a remote consultant simply cannot do properly.

👨‍💻
On-Site Technical Implementation

Some technical controls require hands-on configuration — network segmentation, server hardening, physical media controls. Our Denver-based team can do this in your office without shipping everything to a remote support session.

👥
Staff Training & Interviews

C3PAOs interview your staff during assessments. We prepare your team in-person — walking through likely questions, reviewing procedures, and making sure everyone knows their role in your security program.

⏱
Faster Response When It Matters

FAQ

CMMC Consultant Questions Answered

Do I need a CMMC consultant or can I do this myself?⌄
Technically you can pursue CMMC compliance without a consultant — but most Denver defense contractors find it extremely difficult without expert guidance. The 110 NIST 800-171 controls span 14 domains, require detailed documentation, and need to be implemented correctly to survive a C3PAO assessment. Most companies that attempt DIY compliance either fail their assessment or spend far more time and money than they would have with a consultant.
How is Workplace IT different from other CMMC consultants?⌄
Most CMMC consultants are pure advisors — they tell you what to do and leave implementation to you or another vendor. Workplace IT is both a CMMC consultant and a managed IT provider. We don't just write your SSP and hand it over. We implement the technical controls, manage your systems, and monitor your compliance on an ongoing basis. One partner for the full journey — not a handoff at the implementation stage.
How long does CMMC consulting engagement take?⌄
The initial gap assessment typically takes 2-4 weeks. Full remediation and preparation for a Level 2 C3PAO assessment takes 3-9 months depending on the size of your gaps. Companies with existing IT security programs and some documentation in place move faster. We'll give you a realistic timeline after the initial gap assessment — not an optimistic estimate that sets you up for disappointment.
Can Workplace IT help if we've already started our CMMC journey?⌄
Absolutely. We frequently engage with companies mid-process — reviewing existing SSPs, identifying gaps in prior work, picking up where another consultant left off, or helping companies prepare for an upcoming C3PAO assessment. We'll assess where you are and build a plan from there, not restart from scratch.
What does CMMC consulting cost in Denver?⌄
The initial gap assessment is free. Full consulting engagement costs depend on your environment size, current security posture, and which level you're targeting. We offer fixed-fee project pricing so you know the full cost upfront — no hourly billing surprises. Contact us for a specific quote after your free gap assessment.
