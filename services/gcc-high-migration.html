Common Questions

GCC High Migration FAQ

Who needs Microsoft 365 GCC High?▾
Defense contractors that store, process or transmit Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) or ITAR/export-controlled data and must meet DFARS 252.204-7012 typically need GCC High. Commercial Microsoft 365 does not meet DFARS 7012 requirements for CUI, and GCC (moderate) is generally not sufficient for ITAR data.
What is the difference between GCC and GCC High?▾
GCC runs on Microsoft's US government community cloud and meets FedRAMP Moderate. GCC High is a separate, isolated environment with FedRAMP High-equivalent controls, US-person support staff, and contractual commitments for DFARS 7012 and ITAR. Most defense contractors handling CUI for CMMC Level 2 choose GCC High.
Can you migrate us from commercial Microsoft 365 to GCC High?▾
Yes. Microsoft does not offer an in-place upgrade, so we build a new GCC High tenant and migrate users, mailboxes, files and devices to it. We plan the cutover so email, file shares and printers keep working while users move over.
Do we have to replace our computers?▾
Not always. Devices are usually reset and re-enrolled into GCC High Entra ID using Windows Autopilot and Intune. Some clients buy new computers at the same time, and we can provision them so they arrive ready to use.
How long does a GCC High migration take?▾
Licensing approval from Microsoft or a GCC High reseller can take a few weeks. After that, a typical small or mid-sized business migration is planned and executed over several weeks, with the user cutover itself scheduled to minimize downtime.
Does moving to GCC High make us CMMC compliant?▾
No. GCC High gives you a cloud environment that can support CMMC Level 2 and DFARS 7012, but you still have to configure it correctly and implement and document the NIST SP 800-171 controls. As a Cyber AB Registered Practitioner Organization (RPO), Workplace IT helps with both the tenant build and CMMC readiness.
