Common Questions

AI Automation FAQ

What is AI business process automation?▾
It's using AI and software integration to take repetitive, manual work off your team's plate: moving data between applications, setting up new users and computers, answering routine questions, and similar tasks. The goal is the same result in a fraction of the time, with fewer errors.
What kinds of tasks can you automate?▾
Common examples include user onboarding, computer deployment, workflows between two applications (like moving site survey data into a quoting system), and custom AI agents that answer employee questions from your internal information. If your team repeats the same steps over and over, it's worth a conversation.
Is it safe to give AI access to our company data?▾
It can be when it's designed that way. We give AI only the access it needs for the task, limit agents to approved information sources, and in some cases remove AI from the process entirely once the automation is built. We also pair automation with an AI policy and Data Loss Prevention controls.
What is AI governance, and does my company need it?▾
AI governance is the policy, training, and technical controls that guide how employees use AI. If your team already uses AI tools, you need it. We help you write AI policies, train your users, and set up controls so sensitive information doesn't end up where it shouldn't.
What is Data Loss Prevention (DLP) for AI?▾
DLP watches for sensitive information, such as client data or financial records, being entered into AI tools and other apps. Based on the policy you set, it can alert you or block the action.
We work with the Department of Defense. Can we still use AI?▾
Possibly, with the right guardrails. As a CMMC Registered Practitioner Organization supporting defense industrial base clients, we design AI use around what data it can and can't touch, so automation doesn't create compliance problems. Learn more about our CMMC services.
How do we get started?▾
Tell us about a task your team hates doing. We'll tell you whether it can be automated, what it would take, and how to do it securely. There's no obligation to start.
