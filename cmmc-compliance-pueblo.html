CMMC Pueblo — Common Questions

Do Pueblo manufacturers need CMMC compliance?⌄
Yes — if your Pueblo manufacturing business supplies components, materials, or services under DoD contracts, CMMC requirements apply to you. This includes steel suppliers, fabricators, equipment manufacturers, and logistics companies in the DoD supply chain. The required level (1 or 2) depends on whether your contracts involve FCI only or CUI as well.
How does Workplace IT serve Pueblo businesses?⌄
We provide full CMMC consulting services to Pueblo clients through a combination of remote work and on-site visits. Gap assessments, documentation review, policy writing, and virtual training can all be done remotely. For physical security assessments and hands-on technical work, we schedule on-site visits to Pueblo — approximately 110 miles and under 2 hours from our Denver office.
Is CMMC different for manufacturers vs IT companies?⌄
The 110 NIST 800-171 controls apply equally regardless of industry. However, manufacturers often have unique considerations — operational technology (OT) environments, physical media containing technical drawings, and production floor systems that may touch CUI. We have experience scoping CMMC boundaries for manufacturers to ensure production systems are addressed appropriately without unnecessarily expanding the compliance scope.
What if we subcontract to Fort Carson contractors?⌄
CMMC requirements flow down through the supply chain. If you perform work as a subcontractor under a DoD prime or a Fort Carson support contract that involves CUI, you have CMMC obligations. Many Pueblo businesses discover their subcontractor status creates compliance requirements they weren't aware of — we help you determine exactly what applies to your situation.
How long does CMMC take for a Pueblo industrial company?⌄
Timeline varies based on your starting point. Manufacturers with existing IT departments and some security infrastructure in place typically complete Level 2 preparation in 4-8 months. Smaller shops starting from a basic security baseline may take longer. The initial gap assessment — which is free — will give you a realistic timeline specific to your environment.
