CMMC Boulder — Common Questions

Does CMMC apply to Boulder technology startups with DoD contracts?⌄
Yes — CMMC applies to all companies in the DoD supply chain regardless of size, including early-stage startups. If your Boulder tech company holds or competes for DoD contracts that involve FCI or CUI, CMMC requirements apply to you. SBIR and STTR recipients are a common example — if your grant involves CUI, you need CMMC compliance. The good news is that smaller companies often have simpler environments that are faster and less expensive to bring into compliance.
How does Workplace IT serve Boulder clients?⌄
We provide remote CMMC consulting for most of the engagement — gap assessments, documentation, policy writing, and virtual training sessions. For physical security assessments or hands-on technical work, we can be on-site in Boulder in about 45 minutes from our Denver office. We work with many Boulder clients on a primarily remote basis with occasional on-site visits.
Are CU Boulder research contracts subject to CMMC?⌄
It depends on the contract and the nature of the research. DoD-funded research that generates or involves CUI requires CMMC compliance — this is increasingly common for engineering, aerospace, and computer science research. Basic research with public results (Fundamental Research Exclusion) is generally not subject to CMMC. We help Boulder research organizations determine exactly which of their contracts trigger CMMC requirements.
What makes Boulder defense contractors different from other Colorado cities?⌄
Boulder contractors tend to be more technically sophisticated and often have better baseline security practices than average. However, CMMC requires specific documentation, formal policies, and particular control implementations that go beyond general good security hygiene. Many Boulder companies find they're 60-70% of the way there from a technical standpoint but need significant work on documentation, formal policies, and the specific control evidence that assessors look for.
How long does CMMC take for a Boulder aerospace company?⌄
For Boulder companies with existing IT security programs, CMMC compliance typically takes 2-5 months for Level 2. Companies starting with strong baseline security practices — which is common in Boulder — move through remediation faster. The documentation phase (SSP, policies, evidence collection) is often where the most time is spent regardless of technical readiness.
