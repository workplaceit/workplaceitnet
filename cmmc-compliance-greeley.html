FAQ

CMMC Greeley — Common Questions

How do Greeley businesses know if they need CMMC?⌄
If your Greeley business holds or performs work under DoD contracts — directly or as a subcontractor — you likely have CMMC obligations. The clearest indicators are contract language referencing CMMC, DFARS 252.204-7012 clauses, or your prime contractor notifying you of requirements. If you're unsure, our free gap assessment will determine exactly what applies to your situation.
Does Workplace IT serve Greeley?⌄
Yes. We serve Greeley businesses with a combination of remote consulting and on-site support when needed. Most CMMC work — gap assessments, documentation, policy writing, virtual training — is done remotely. For physical security assessments or hands-on technical work, Greeley is approximately 60 miles from our Denver office and we can schedule on-site visits as needed.
We've never done CMMC before — where do we start?⌄
Start with our free gap assessment. It takes 2-4 weeks and gives you a complete picture of where you stand against the 110 NIST 800-171 controls. You'll receive a prioritized gap report, a realistic remediation timeline, and a fixed-fee quote for the full compliance project. Most first-time CMMC companies find this assessment extremely clarifying — it turns an overwhelming requirement into a manageable project plan.
Can you help us respond to a prime contractor asking about CMMC?⌄
Absolutely. We frequently help companies respond to CMMC questionnaires from prime contractors, understand what their specific contracts require, and put together a compliance roadmap to share with their prime. If a prime is asking about your CMMC status, that's a signal to act — we can help you respond honestly and put a credible plan in place.
What does CMMC compliance cost for a Greeley small business?⌄
The gap assessment is free. Full project costs for small businesses (under 25 employees) with straightforward environments typically range from $15,000 to $40,000 for Level 2 preparation, depending on gap size. Ongoing compliance monitoring is a flat monthly fee. We provide a fixed-fee quote after the gap assessment so you know the exact cost before committing to anything.
