Client Referral Program

Refer a Business. Earn $500.

Workplace IT is growing, and much of that growth comes from clients like you. Introduce us to a company that could use a better IT partner. Once we hold a qualifying meeting with them, we'll send you $500. They don't have to sign with us; the meeting is enough.

How it works

1
Submit a referral
Fill out the form with your details and the company you're referring.
2
We reach out
We contact them and set up a discovery meeting.
3
You get $500
Paid within 30 days of the qualifying meeting. No limit on referrals.

Who's a good fit?

  • Businesses with 10+ employees
  • Companies frustrated with their current IT support, or with none
  • Defense contractors working toward CMMC compliance
  • Financial services firms with strict security and compliance requirements
  • Teams that need help with Microsoft 365, security, or networking
  • Offices that are moving, expanding, or need new data and fiber cabling
  • Businesses looking to add or upgrade security cameras
  • Businesses that want guidance on using AI safely and productively

The fine print: a qualifying meeting is a scheduled discovery meeting with a decision-maker at a company with 10+ employees that isn't currently a Workplace IT client or already in conversations with us. One payout per referred company. Paid within 30 days of the meeting.
