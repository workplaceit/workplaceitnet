CMMC Fort Collins — Common Questions

Do CSU research contractors need CMMC compliance?⌄
Yes — if your CSU-affiliated research involves DoD funding and generates or handles Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), CMMC requirements apply. This includes SBIR and STTR recipients, research labs with DoD grants, and companies that license or commercialize DoD-funded technology. The level of CMMC required depends on the sensitivity of the research data involved.
Does Workplace IT serve Fort Collins businesses?⌄
Yes. While we're based in Denver, we provide full CMMC consulting services to Fort Collins businesses. Most of the CMMC engagement — gap assessments, documentation review, policy writing, and virtual training — can be done remotely. For physical security assessments or hands-on technical work, our team can be on-site in Fort Collins in about an hour from our Denver office.
What if we're a subcontractor to a prime in Colorado Springs or Denver?⌄
CMMC requirements flow down through the supply chain. If the prime contractor you support handles CUI and your work touches that information, you have CMMC obligations regardless of your own location. We frequently work with Fort Collins subcontractors whose prime is based in Colorado Springs, Denver, or even out of state.
How is CMMC different for research organizations vs manufacturers?⌄
The 110 NIST 800-171 controls apply equally regardless of industry, but how they're implemented looks different. Research organizations tend to have more dispersed data environments — laptops in multiple locations, cloud research platforms, external collaborators — which requires careful scoping of what's in your CMMC boundary. Workplace IT has experience scoping and documenting research environments specifically.
What does CMMC compliance cost for a Fort Collins research company?⌄
The initial gap assessment is free. Full project costs depend on your environment size and current security posture. Research organizations with 5-25 people typically find the engagement more manageable than larger manufacturers because the environment is smaller — though data scoping can be complex. We provide fixed-fee pricing after the gap assessment.
